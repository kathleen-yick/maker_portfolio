description (as many lines as you like; blank line = new paragraph):

To increase copper regeneration rates from scrap yards back to manufacturers,
this project aimed to collect large amounts of data to train an ML to automate \
copper idenfication.

Therefore, we needed a portable data collection box that could take data efficiently
and accurately at an actual scrapyard.
In preparation: I conducted 15+ interviews with scrapyard operators and metal recyclers to identify
challenges in scrap identification and purchasing; tested and optimized MIS coil geometry; and
fabricated a robust, controlled testing environment.

During site testing, we were able to scan at a rate of 5 per minute (including manual
feeding) and added ~600 unique scraps to our ML.
