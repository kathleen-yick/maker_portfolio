EXAMPLE PROJECT NOTES
=====================
Copy this folder for each new project. Rename the folder to the project
(lower-case, no spaces, e.g.  bridge  or  solar-tracker).

Photos in this folder are shown in number order. 1 is the thumbnail.
Use 1 to 6 files: 1.jpg 2.jpg 3.png 4.pdf ...
(.jpg / .jpeg, .png and .pdf all work, in any mix.)

Fill in what you know below. Anything you leave blank, Claude drafts
from the photos, and you can refine it later in index.html.

title:        B
category:     design       (engineering | design | fun; for two columns write: engineering, design)
year:         2023
role:         Design, fabrication, photography
tools:        Sewing machine, design, prototyping
link:         (a GitHub / Behance / other URL, or leave blank)
featured:     no                 (yes = also shown on the About Me page)

blurb (one or two sentences for under the thumbnail):
  A birthday dress for my sister she asked me to design and photograph

description (as many lines as you like; blank line = new paragraph):
  This custom dress was inspired by fashions icons like Rhinanna and uses
  black satin, imitation pearls, and string. The process went through multiple
  iterations, including a corset alternative to the top, and was tested with
  client (aka sister) throughout the entire fabrication. Definitely learned a
  lot with this prooject!
